I received my B.Eng. in Computer Science from Yao Class at Tsinghua University's Institute for Interdisciplinary Information Sciences. I have broad interests in theoretical computer science, particularly in data structures and approximation algorithms.

During the spring of 2025, I had a great time visiting [William Kuszmaul](https://sites.google.com/site/williamkuszmaul) at CMU.
